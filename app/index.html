    Roadmap to the Platinum

    Around 200 hours, in one playthrough, no online trophies. The order below is built from the official trophy descriptions: story first, side stories second, free-roam activities and crime third, collectibles and the 100% checklist last. Nothing needs a second playthrough if you keep manual saves — except the one missable trophy, flagged in the Missables tab.

    Save rules that protect the whole run

    • No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — rotate the rest.
    • Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats switch off trophies and saving.
    • Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (see Missables) — the only spot known to disappear.
    • Do Race Against Yago the moment it appears in Chapter 3 — it is time-sensitive and its strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks.

    Sources: Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List; Exophase — official list.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is confirmed missable. The rest of the list stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist — but the save rules in the Roadmap tab still apply, because there is no mission replay.
